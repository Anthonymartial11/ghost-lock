Ghost + Lock

Two apps. One job: make you hard to find and hard to hit.

Ghost
Clean up the past — erase your footprint ›
Lock
Protect the present — block what’s coming ›

Put them on your phone & Mac

iPhone: open Ghost or Lock in Safari → tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen. Do it for each app. Real icon, full screen, works offline.

Mac: open the app in Safari → File menu → Add to Dock.

Important: set up the password inside the installed app, not in the Safari tab — installed apps keep their own separate storage. Each app has its own independent lock: two apps, two vaults, so a problem in one can never touch the other.
